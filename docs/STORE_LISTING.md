# CORZA — store listing (Google Play)

**App name (title, max 30):** CORZA – AI Fitness & Boxing  (27 characters)

**Short description (max 80):**
Your camera coach for squats, push-ups and boxing basics. Private, on your phone.

**Full description (draft):**
CORZA is a training companion that watches your movement through your phone's camera and coaches you in real time.

• Fitness: squats, hip hinges, push-ups, lunges, mobility and stretching, with rep counting where it is reliable.
• Boxing basics: stance, jab, cross, guard return and footwork drills — non-contact, shadow practice only.
• A 3D coach shows every movement before you try it.
• Live cues tied to clear rules, shown with how sure the camera is. If the view is poor, CORZA says so instead of guessing.
• Pose tracking runs on your phone. No video is uploaded or saved.
• Works without an account. Training plans, reminders and your progress history stay on the device.

CORZA is an educational practice companion for adults. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or prevent
injury, and does not replace a qualified trainer or instructor. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or unusual discomfort.
