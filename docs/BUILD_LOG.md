### 2026-11-19 / Push-up clips + boxing form check (experimental)
- **Push-up:** `pushup_down` / `pushup_up` for both coaches, built in `build_coach_mesh.py`: the body pivots about
  the toes as a straight plank (shoulder height from arm length at the top to ~0.20 m at the bottom), hands planted

- **Boxing form check** (`core/movement/BoxingFormMonitor`): rule-based, pixel-space, normalised by shoulder width.
  Stance: hands low (both wrists clearly below the shoulder line), feet narrow (< 0.75 shoulder widths) or very wide
  (> 2.1). Jab: counts lead-hand extensions (reach ratio > 0.86, min 400 ms apart), flags the rear hand dropping
  during the extension and the hand not returning to guard within 1.5 s. A cue needs the rule true in ≥ 70 % of the
  last second of frames and is held ≥ 2.5 s; missing/low-visibility landmarks or tracking off → "Form check paused".
  Wording is always "possible form adjustment"; never scores power, speed or injury risk. Session screen shows it
  over the camera for the stance and jab steps, labelled experimental, with a jab count for the jab.
- Tests: 9 new monitor tests (good guard → no cue; hands low / narrow feet → cue after a second; a single bad frame
  never advises; low visibility and tracking off → paused; jabs counted once per extension; rear-hand drop and slow
  return flagged) — run locally with the bundled Kotlin compiler + JUnit 4: 9/9; planner test for the form drills.
- Thresholds are hypotheses for coach review and device testing; nothing measured on a phone yet.

## On-device look fix (2026-11-19, from owner's phone screenshots)
- Owner: coach "looks like a disaster" on the lesson page. Causes found: model filled only ~1/3 of the viewer,
  no anti-aliasing (jagged), near-mirror baked roughness (median 0.11 → oily plastic), short bake rays leaving dark
  speckled seam lines on muscles, and no exported tangents (Filament's own tangents disagree with Blender's
  MikkTSpace bake → visible normal-map seams).
- Fixes: model scaled ×1.38 about the unit-cube centre; 4× MSAA + FXAA, dynamic resolution off, SSAO, AgX (punchy)
  tone mapping; ModelViewer's overhead 100 klx sun reduced to 45 klx and tilted to the front; ambient lowered so
  the key light shapes the body; roughness remapped to 0.45–0.95; bake cage 2 cm / rays 6 cm; tangents exported
  (degenerate ones patched in `fix_tangents`). Validator: 0 errors. Needs a device check.

## Workouts survive the app being closed (2026-11-20)
- `SessionSnapshot` (pure Kotlin, JSON) + `ActiveSessionStore` (app-private `noBackupFilesDir/active_session.json`,
  atomic tmp+rename, epoch guard so a late background write can't revive a saved/discarded session). Only
  timer/step/result numbers are stored — no camera data.
- `SessionViewModel` writes the latest state on every change (conflated, IO), restores it when Android reopens
  the session screen, and continues **paused** (closed-app time is not counted) with a "Picked up where you left
  off" note. Cleared on save, Done/Discard, starting another session, or Reset app data; expires after 24 h.
- Home shows an "Unfinished workout" card (Resume / Discard) or "Workout not saved yet" (Review & save).
- Tests: SessionSnapshotTest (round trip, resume paused, finished stays finished, rejects bad/tampered data,
  24 h expiry) — 17/17 session tests pass locally. Process-death behaviour still needs a device check
  (Developer options → "Don't keep activities", or `adb shell am kill`).

## More natural motion + new visual identity (2026-11-20)
- Owner: animations "too stiff", app needs a professional, unique redesign.
- Squat is now motion capture (CMU 13_29 f1036–1128, arms reaching forward for balance), softened 44 % toward
  standing (`SOFTEN`) so the knees bottom out near 95° instead of a full deep squat — the performer's timing,
  hip-back/torso-lean coordination and balance are kept.
- March rebuilt as a layered gait cycle (75 steps/min): knee rises with a held top and quicker drop (~95° knee),
  pelvis shifts over and drops toward the standing leg, ribcage counter-rotates, bent-elbow arm swing trailing the
  shoulder, head level, small landing dip, breathing.
- Identity: light cool "gym floor" ground (#ECEFF3), white surfaces, ink type; cobalt = fitness/primary,
  glove red = fighting; workout screen runs on a dark "night" scheme. Fonts: Big Shoulders Display (headings,
  numbers) + Schibsted Grotesk (text), both SIL OFL (licences in assets/licenses). Barlow removed.
- Signature: the exercise name set huge and faint behind the coach — on Home's "Today" block, lesson cards,
  the session summary, and live behind the 3D coach (Filament view now renders with a transparent background).
- Copy: sentence-case labels, no "A · B" strings, plain-language lengths ("2 min").
