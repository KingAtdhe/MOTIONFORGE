### 2026-11-19 / Push-up clips + boxing form check (experimental)
- **Push-up:** `pushup_down` / `pushup_up` for both coaches, built in `build_coach_mesh.py`: the body pivots about
  the toes as a straight plank (shoulder height from arm length at the top to ~0.20 m at the bottom), hands planted

- **Boxing form check** (`core/movement/BoxingFormMonitor`): rule-based, pixel-space, normalised by shoulder width.
  Stance: hands low (both wrists clearly below the shoulder line), feet narrow (< 0.75 shoulder widths) or very wide
  (> 2.1). Jab: counts lead-hand extensions (reach ratio > 0.86, min 400 ms apart), flags the rear hand dropping
  during the extension and the hand not returning to guard within 1.5 s. A cue needs the rule true in ≥ 70 % of the
  last second of frames and is held ≥ 2.5 s; missing/low-visibility landmarks or tracking off → "Form check paused".
  Wording is always "possible form adjustment"; never scores power, speed or injury risk. Session screen shows it
  over the camera for the stance and jab steps, labelled experimental, with a jab count for the jab.
- Tests: 9 new monitor tests (good guard → no cue; hands low / narrow feet → cue after a second; a single bad frame
  never advises; low visibility and tracking off → paused; jabs counted once per extension; rear-hand drop and slow
  return flagged) — run locally with the bundled Kotlin compiler + JUnit 4: 9/9; planner test for the form drills.
- Thresholds are hypotheses for coach review and device testing; nothing measured on a phone yet.

## Workouts survive the app being closed (2026-11-20)
- `SessionSnapshot` (pure Kotlin, JSON) + `ActiveSessionStore` (app-private `noBackupFilesDir/active_session.json`,
  atomic tmp+rename, epoch guard so a late background write can't revive a saved/discarded session). Only
  timer/step/result numbers are stored — no camera data.
- `SessionViewModel` writes the latest state on every change (conflated, IO), restores it when Android reopens
  the session screen, and continues **paused** (closed-app time is not counted) with a "Picked up where you left
  off" note. Cleared on save, Done/Discard, starting another session, or Reset app data; expires after 24 h.
- Home shows an "Unfinished workout" card (Resume / Discard) or "Workout not saved yet" (Review & save).

## Pocket fins and skin streaks (2026-11-20)
- Pocket "fins" on the male coach: Tripo's skinning leaked small finger/hand weights onto the shorts where the
  hands rest against the thighs, so those patches followed the hands whenever the arms moved. `strip_hand_leaks`
  drops hand/finger weights from any vertex that mostly belongs to legs/hips/spine (1329 verts on the male, 0 on the
  female). `fix_shorts` (male, `--fix-shorts`): shorts cloth (found by texture colour, excluding the gloves) above the
  knee moves off the shin bones onto the thigh, the hip-side panels ride 70 % with the pelvis, and pelvis/thigh weights
  are blended with spatial neighbours (the mesh is split at every UV seam, so neighbours by distance).
  Squat check: anomalous stretched vertices 7 → 2.
- Painted highlight streaks: `skin_cleanup.py` finds skin by hue/saturation, estimates the local skin colour with a
  masked blur and replaces pale/over-bright strokes inside skin areas (~3 % of each texture), keeping muscle shading.

## 2026-11-21 — Coach animation refinement: root-motion bug fixed, all 14 clips re-authored
**Audit (tools: per-clip joint/foot metrics on the exported GLB, edge-stretch audit, front/side/three-quarter renders).**
- Root cause of the "weird" squat: the floor-contact pass measured the stand-in mesh, whose feet do not follow the rig on the Tripo coaches. It cancelled the hips' vertical motion, so in the shipped GLB the hips stayed still and the feet rose 41 cm (and the hinge sank 23 cm into the floor). Confirmed with a spec-exact glTF evaluation.
- The recorded takes themselves were not demonstration quality: jab elbow never past 114 deg, hinge knees at 127 deg with heels lifting, push-up elbows uneven (62-171 vs 82-180), march foot sliding 23-55 cm, head pitched 40 deg down in squat and march.

**Changes.**
- `rigged_coach.floor_contact`: floor contact is now measured on the real skinned mesh after the transfer. Largest correction is now 1-3 mm.
- `tools/blender/authored_clips.py` (new): every clip is built from explicit biomechanical rules with IK (planted feet pinned at the ball, knees over toes, neutral spine, slower lowering than rising, holds at top and bottom, each rep ends in its start pose). Recorded takes remain available with `MF_LEGACY_MOCAP=1`.
  - Squat: shoulder-width, toes 20 deg out, thighs to parallel, shins ~33 deg, torso ~40 deg, arms rise as counterbalance. 2.0 s down, hold, 1.45 s up.
  - Hip hinge: hips travel back, shins vertical, knees soft (~22 deg), flat back to ~64 deg, neck in line, hands track the legs.
  - Push-up: straight plank pivoting on the balls of the feet, hands planted slightly wider than shoulders (palms 3-8 mm above the floor in the final mesh), elbows ~45 deg, 170 deg at the top, shoulder blades spread/retract.
  - March: pinned stance foot, weight shift over the standing leg, opposite arm swing, chest counter-rotation.
  - Idle: weight shift, breathing, relaxed arms; seamless 4 s loop.
  - Boxing (orthodox): one shared stance for stance / jab / cross / guard return / footwork; full straight-line extension with shoulder cover, rear-foot pivot and hip rotation on the cross; push-step footwork.
- `rigged_coach.transfer`: toes stay flat on the floor when the heel is raised.
- App: `DemoSequence` plays a footwork step with its return step so the drill loops without a position jump (unit test added).
- The two coaches share technique; stance width, tempo (+4 %), arm carriage and sway differ slightly with build.

**Verified in Blender on the exported GLBs:** planted-foot slide 0.0-0.3 cm on all stationary clips, worst edge stretch < 0.8 %, no IK over-reach, down/up pairs meet exactly.
